# health-tips

**Status: DRAFT, awaiting approval.** No sources could be opened in this session (see Facts and sources). The script makes habit suggestions only, with no statistics or medical claims.

## Hook (max 3 s)

"Three habits your body notices first." (6 words, about 2.5 s)

## Beats

1. Hook card on screen: "3 small habits" (0-3 s).
2. Habit 1: drink a glass of water when you wake up (shown as a glass icon with a pop-in).
3. Habit 2: take a short walk after meals instead of sitting straight down (shown as a walking figure or the avatar standing up).
4. Habit 3: go to bed and get up at about the same time each day (shown as a clock card).
5. Twist card: "Pick one, not all three."
6. CTA end card with the comment keyword.

## Twist

You do not need all three today. Pick one, keep it for a week, then add the next.

## CTA (comment keyword)

Keyword: **HABITS**. Last 3-4 s: "Comment HABITS and I will send you the checklist." Only use this line once the checklist exists and you have agreed to send it.
