## Description
100 balls, numbered 1 to 100, follow one rule every time they hit a wall:
even → cut in half, odd → triple it and add 1. Reach 1 and you're free.

Ball #27 climbs all the way to 9,232 before it comes back down.

This is the Collatz conjecture (Lothar Collatz, 1937). Every starting number checked by computers,
up to 2⁶⁸, eventually reaches 1, but nobody has ever proven that every number does.
Paul Erdős said: "Mathematics may not be ready for such problems."

The simulation is real: every bounce applies the rule, and the numbers on the balls are exact.
The last few balls are shown at 2.5× speed.
