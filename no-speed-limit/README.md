# No Speed Limit

"Can a No-Speed-Limit Ball Deal 1 Quintillion Damage in 60 Seconds?" The third video in the futuristic
arena series.

- The giant ball has **1 quintillion HP (10¹⁸)**. Each hit deals 1 damage and makes the small ball
  **1.6% faster, with no limit**.
- Real physics runs until the ball passes about 300 m/s (around hit 360), when frames can't show anything new.
- From there the page **zooms into time**. The gaps between hits shrink by the same factor each time, so
  infinitely many hits fit before a fixed moment, T∞ (Zeno's paradox). The counters come from that exact
  formula: hits, speed (it passes light speed), hits per second, and HP.
- Answer: **YES**. 1 quintillion hits land before about 54.7s, and infinitely many by T∞.

On load ("LOADING…"), the page picks a starting pattern where T∞ lands between 54.5 and 57.8 seconds.

**Voiceover:** the page is silent apart from sound effects. To have it play your own voice lines, put
`vo-intro.mp3` (plays at the start) and `vo-outro.mp3` (plays 1.3s after "YES.") next to `index.html`. Or set
`USE_BROWSER_VOICE = true` in the script for a built-in read-out.

## Record it
Start your screen recorder, open `index.html` and tap **TAP TO START**. About 62 seconds to "YES.", and
**RUN IT AGAIN** appears 9 seconds later, so trim it off.
